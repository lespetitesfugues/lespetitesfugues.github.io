LPF Explorer · Current release

Frequently Asked Questions

Sensible answers. The occasional raised eyebrow.

Everything you wanted to know about LPF Explorer, including a few things you were probably too polite to ask.

Do I need to import my photographs?

No. Point Explorer at your existing archive. Your folders are already doing a perfectly respectable job of being folders.

Can I use more than one archive?

Yes. Add several archives and switch between them, including archives stored on external drives. Explorer keeps each archive's collections and metadata information separate.

Does it create another photo library?

No proprietary catalogue. Explorer uses local indexes for speed, but your photographs stay where you put them.

Do I need an account or cloud service?

No account or cloud service is required. Photographs and visual-search descriptions stay on your Mac. The optional AI model download and software update check use the internet, but do not upload photographs.

Does my archive need a particular folder structure?

No. Year/date folders work well, but Explorer does not insist on them.

What about RAW+JPEG pairs?

Associated RAW and JPEG files can be treated as one photograph, without moving or modifying the originals.

What's the difference between Metadata and Visual Search?

Metadata Search finds words in filenames and XMP fields. Visual Search lets you describe a scene, even if it has no matching keywords. Both work in the selected archive, folder or collection, and each mode remembers its own search text.

Is Visual Search really processed locally?

Yes. The SigLIP 2 Core ML model analyses images and text on your Mac. Your photographs and search descriptions are not sent to an external AI service.

Do I have to install an AI model?

Not at all. Browsing, Metadata Search and collections work without it. Visual Search offers a one-time download of about 750 MB from Hugging Face, with your confirmation and download progress. You can also install compatible model files from a local folder. Allow at least 2 GB of free space for installation.

Do I have to install the model for each archive?

No. Install it once for LPF Explorer on this Mac. Each archive then gets its own automatically maintained visual index.

What do the Visual Search similarity scores mean?

They rank the matches for a description. Higher values generally indicate closer matches relative to other results, but they are not percentages or probabilities that an image is correct.

Will adding or changing photographs require another full visual scan?

Normally not. Explorer incrementally updates the visual index when photographs are added, changed or removed. You can continue browsing while indexing runs.

Can I combine search filters?

Yes, in Metadata mode. Combine text, ratings, capture dates, Finder tags and technical EXIF conditions. Visual mode uses the selected archive, folder or collection as its scope; its normal metadata filters are disabled.

Smart collection or manual collection: which is which?

Smart collections save metadata search criteria; manual collections save your selection. Neither copies or moves photographs. Visual descriptions are temporary, not saved smart-collection rules.

Can I organize collections into groups?

Yes, one level of groups for each collection type. Enough organisation to be useful, not enough to need an organisational chart.

Does deleting a collection delete my photographs?

No. A collection contains references, not photographs. Your originals remain in their folders.

Can Explorer edit XMP metadata?

No. Descriptive metadata is read-only here. Use LPF Organizer to edit titles, descriptions, keywords and other XMP fields.

Can I change Finder workflow tags in Explorer?

No. Explorer filters by Finder tags; use Finder to change them.

Can I use an external photo editor?

Yes. Open photographs with other applications or send selected photographs to LPF Organizer for metadata editing.

Do I need LPF Organizer?

No. Explorer works independently. LPF Organizer is needed only for the handoff feature.

What if my archive is offline?

Cached metadata, collections and previously generated thumbnails can remain visible. Reconnect the drive to open originals; Explorer then checks the archive for changes automatically.

Is there a subscription?

No. We briefly considered charging you monthly to search your own folders, but it seemed rather impolite. Donations are optional.

What happens when I first open the downloaded app?

LPF Explorer is signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple. macOS may show its standard downloaded-app confirmation. Choose Open; there should be no need to bypass Gatekeeper.
